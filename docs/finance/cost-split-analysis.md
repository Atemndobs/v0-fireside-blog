## How to Use the Finance Tracker

The finance tracker shows:

1. **Expenses Tab:** All Shield payments ($3,349.33 total)
   - Category: "shield-editor"
   - Person: "atem" (who paid)
   - Notes include cost split breakdown

2. **Contributions Tab:** Payments from team members
   - Eunice's 4 contributions totaling $991.22
   - Anyang's contributions (when made)

3. **Dashboard:** Real-time calculations
   - Total expenses vs total contributions
   - Balance showing who owes what
   - Monthly breakdown charts

### To Track New Payments

When Eunice or Anyang makes a payment:
1. Go to `/finance/add`
2. Type: **Contribution**
3. Category: **contribution**
4. Person: **eunice** or **anyang**
5. Amount: Payment amount
6. Description: "Reimbursement for Shield editor costs"
7. Notes: Include payment method/details

The dashboard will automatically update outstanding balances.
